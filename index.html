    ABOUT US

    We make brains smile.

    Eduplica is a small, independent game studio. We make friendly brain games that aim to relax you, yet challenge you, and spark a little joy with every tap.

    Eduplica is a brand of Shabake OÜ, based in Estonia.

    • Our mission

      Keep the classic heart of sudoku, and make every puzzle feel like yours.

    • Who we are

      A small team of friends and trusted partners who are passionate about overall user experience.

    • What drives us

      Simple ideas, clean design and happy players. Brain training should feel like play, not homework.

    • How we design

      Fair puzzles and clear rules, for a quick break or deep focus. Every session should feel good.
